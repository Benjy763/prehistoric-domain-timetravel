**1. Current concept — site & channel**

- Paleoart isn't just illustration — it's staging prehistoric life across mediums.
- Site = curated showcase (illustration, 3D, video, VR, all in one place).
- Channel = looser space, following my own process across different tools, paleoart, gaming, short films, toy photography, compositing.
- Dinosaurs always made tech progress to serve art
- Both scientifically grounded work AND fictional/pop-culture takes (JP-style, adventure-style stuff) — same passion, different flavors.

**2. Creative rhythm**

- No fixed schedule, no content calendar.
- I pick whatever medium I feel like on a given day — toy photo, JWE3, VR scene, compositing.
- Videos come after the creation, not during — talking about what I made, not filming the process live. Keeps the creative time relaxed.

**3. Reorganizing / the two collaborators**

- Spent time automating and streamlining how the site runs — adding artists, CMS, all much simpler now, just a few clicks.
- Because of that, I don't *need* help to keep it running — but I offered two people from the community the chance to get more involved if they want: one on Discord, one on Instagram/artist outreach.
- Not offloading what I don't want to do — just opening the door to share the project more, since it can already run without them.

**4. New YouTube format**

- Simple setup, no scripts.
- Quick notes on the topic, then I just talk.
- Pre-selected clips/images from what I've already made as visual support.
- Point is to keep it light — share the process and thinking, without turning it into a production.
